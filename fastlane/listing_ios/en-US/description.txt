Google Maps can remember the places you go — that is your Timeline. This app opens your Timeline file, shows your trips on a map and saves them as GPX, KML, GeoJSON or CSV files for other apps (Strava, Garmin, Google Earth, Excel…).

Your Timeline file is read on this device and never uploaded. The app does not record your location or sign in to Google; only the map background is loaded from the internet.

BEFORE YOU START
• TIMELINE IS RECORDED BY GOOGLE MAPS, in the app signed in to your Google account. This app reads a file you save from it; it records nothing itself.
• TIMELINE HAS TO BE ON. Google saves only the days since it was turned on, and no app can add the days before that.
• Google Maps needs your location. Google’s setup asks for location access “Always” (Settings app → Google Maps → Location); with less, Timeline may record only part of your day.
• Each phone keeps its own Timeline. History recorded on another phone is not on this iPhone: export it on that phone, or import Google’s backup here (Your Timeline → cloud icon).

GET YOUR FILE IN 3 STEPS
1. In Google Maps tap your profile picture → Settings → Location & Privacy → Export Timeline data.
2. Tap Save to Files and save the file to On My iPhone or iCloud Drive.
3. Tap Choose Timeline file on the main screen and find the file. It is usually called Timeline.json.

Not sure yet? Tap “Try it with sample data”. Made-up trips: see what the app does before exporting your own.

The Help screen in the app explains every step in simple words, including how to turn Timeline on.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See your trips on a map
• Pick dates: last 7, 30 or 90 days, or everything
• Export to GPX (Strava, Garmin, Komoot and most GPS apps), KML (Google Earth), GeoJSON (QGIS, web maps) or CSV (Excel, Numbers)
• Save to Files or send with the share sheet
• Also opens older Google files: Android Timeline.json, Semantic Location History, Records.json

PRIVATE
Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Your location history is never uploaded: no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads. The internet is used only to load the map. Open source.

Timeline Exporter is an independent app, not affiliated with Google.
