Google Maps can remember the places you go — that is your Timeline. This app opens your Timeline file, shows your trips on a map and saves them as GPX, KML, GeoJSON or CSV files for other apps (Strava, Garmin, Google Earth, Excel…).

This app does not track you and does not sign in to Google. It only reads the file you save from Google Maps, and everything stays on this device.

⚠️ IMPORTANT — CHECK THIS FIRST
• YOUR LOCATION HISTORY COMES FROM YOUR GOOGLE ACCOUNT. You need the Google Maps app, signed in with your Google account.
• TIMELINE MUST BE TURNED ON IN GOOGLE MAPS. Google saves only the days since it was turned on — if it was off, there is nothing to export.
• Google Maps needs location access set to “Always” (Settings app → Google Maps → Location), otherwise Timeline records very little.
• Timeline is kept on the phone that recorded it. If your history was recorded on another phone, save the file on that phone.

GET YOUR FILE IN 3 STEPS
1. In Google Maps tap your profile picture → Settings → Location & Privacy → Export Timeline data.
2. Tap Save to Files and save the file to On My iPhone or iCloud Drive.
3. Tap Choose Timeline file on the main screen and find the file. It is usually called location-history.json.

Not sure yet? Tap “Try it with sample data”. Made-up trips: see what the app does before exporting your own.

The Help screen in the app explains every step in simple words, including how to turn Timeline on.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• See your trips on a map
• Pick dates: last 7, 30 or 90 days, or everything
• Export to GPX (Strava, Garmin, Komoot and most GPS apps), KML (Google Earth), GeoJSON (QGIS, web maps) or CSV (Excel, Numbers)
• Save to Files or send with the share sheet
• Also opens older Google files: Android Timeline.json, Semantic Location History, Records.json

PRIVATE
Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad. Your location history is never uploaded: no account, no servers, no analytics, no ads. The internet is used only to load the map. Free and open source.
